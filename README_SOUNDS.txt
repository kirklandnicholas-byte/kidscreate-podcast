KIDSCREATE V5 SOUND LIBRARY — 31 ORIGINAL AUDIO EFFECTS

Deployment: Upload the CONTENTS of this folder to the ROOT of your existing GitHub kidscreate-podcast repository, preserving the sounds/ directory. Commit. Netlify deploys automatically.

Open: https://kidscreate-studio.netlify.app/sounds.html (once your GitHub update is deployed).

Usage: In Sound Effects, choose a category and listen. Click Add to podcast. Then open studio.html and go to Edit & listen to reorder the sound clip. If the Studio was already open in another tab, reload it to see the new clip. The final WAV export includes these audio clips in sequence. Effects can be downloaded individually too.

IMPORTANT LIMITATION: This is a CLIP SEQUENCER, not a multitrack mixer. SFX are placed before/between/after voice recordings and do not play underneath a voice clip. True overlapping tracks and volume/fades would need an additional mixer feature.

All effects were created procedurally specifically for this package. These are artistic approximations, not real animal vocalizations, actual crowds, or authentic field recordings. WAV, 22050 Hz, mono, 16-bit PCM. No third party sound library was used.

The sounds gallery itself is offline-capable after downloading the package, but the Add to podcast feature uses fetch() and requires hosting via HTTPS/localhost. Use Netlify for a complete test.

For student privacy: these sounds do not require login, and adding a sound uses local browser storage. Saving and public sharing are NOT connected to Supabase in this version. Do not upload real student information or recordings until user authentication, parental consent and moderation are verified.
